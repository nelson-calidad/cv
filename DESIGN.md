# Mercury — Style Reference
> Alpine banking at blue hour (Adapted for Nelson Notario Professional Portfolio)

Source measurements and aesthetic guidelines referenced from Mercury / Refero design architecture.

## Adapted Identity Tokens (Executive Light Corporate Theme)

- **Canvas:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure White Canvas)
- **Elevated Card Surface:** `#F6F8F9` (Subtle Fog Surface) / `#FFFFFF` with hairline `#E5ECEF` border
- **Primary Accent Action:** `#1D5060` (Petroleum Blue — single chromatic gravitational center)
- **Primary Text:** `#192B33` (Deep Ink)
- **Muted / Secondary Text:** `#64767E` (Neutral Slate / Ash)
- **Hairline Borders:** `#E5ECEF`
- **Interactive Pill Radius:** `32px` - `40px` (Buttons, tags, navigation pills)
- **Card Radius:** `12px` - `14px`
- **Elevation:** Flat, shadowless, structural separation via value contrast and subtle 1px hairline borders
- **Typography:**
  - Display / Headings: `Manrope` with intermediate weight (500–650), architectural tracking (`-0.02em` to `0.01em`), line-height 1.15
  - Body & UI: `DM Sans` at weight 400 with 1.55–1.65 line-height
